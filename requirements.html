FAQs

What documents are needed?

You need birth certificate, report card, and proof of residence.

When is the deadline?

All admission documents must be submitted by July 15th to be considered.

Can I submit online?

Yes, submissions can be made through the school's online portal or in person at the admissions office.

Is there an application fee?

No, there is no fee required for submitting admission documents.

Who can I contact for help?

Contact the admissions office via phone or email for assistance.

What if I miss the deadline?

Late submissions may not be accepted, but contact admissions immediately to discuss options.
