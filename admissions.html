FAQs

Who can apply?

Any student called to grow as a Christian leader is welcome to apply.

What programs are offered?

We offer Bachelor of Arts in Asian Studies, Bachelor of Arts in Theology, Bachelor of Christian Education and Bachelor of Physical Education.

We also offer Master of Arts in Christian Education, Master of Arts in Theological Studies, and Doctor of Ministry.

How do I apply?

Start by filling out our online application form.

Are scholarships available?

Yes, scholarships are offered based on need and spiritual commitment.

Can international students apply?

Absolutely, we welcome students from around the world called to serve through Christ.
