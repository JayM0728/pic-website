Bachelor of Physical Education

Why choose Bachelor of Physical Education?

Train. Lead. Inspire. Build a Career in Fitness, Sports, and Education.

Become a Future Leader in Sports and Physical Education

The Bachelor of Physical Education (BPE) at Philippine International College is designed for students who are passionate about sports, fitness, movement sciences, and teaching. This program equips future educators, coaches, and trainers with the knowledge, skills, and discipline needed to lead in the growing fields of sports and physical education.

A Strong Blend of Physical Education and Holistic Formation

Students receive comprehensive training in both physical education and foundational teaching principles. Subjects include:

  • Foundations of Physical Education
  • Anatomy and Physiology
  • Sports Science and Coaching
  • Exercise and Fitness Training
  • Motor Learning and Movement Skills
  • Curriculum Development in PE
  • Teaching Strategies in Physical Activities
  • Classroom and Sports Management
  • Child and Adolescent Development
  • Values, Discipline, and Character Formation

With this strong combination of theory and practice, graduates become well-rounded PE educators who promote health, discipline, and excellence in every learner.

Practical Training and Classroom Experience

Students grow through actual teaching exposure and ministry involvement such as:

  • Practice teaching in Christian schools
  • Sunday school teaching
  • Outreach programs
  • Curriculum workshops
  • Church and community educational ministries

These experiences build confidence and readiness for real-world teaching.

Pathways to Local and International Careers

Graduates can pursue roles such as:

  • Physical Education Teacher
  • Sports Coach or Trainer
  • Fitness Instructor / Gym Coach
  • Athletic Program Coordinator
  • School Sports Moderator
  • Wellness and Health Advocate
  • Recreation and Sports Facilitator
  • Strength and Conditioning Assistant
  • Community Sports Program Worker
  • Graduate Studies in Physical Education, Sports Science, or Education

Eligible to Take the LET (Licensure Examination for Teachers)

Graduates of the Bachelor of Physical Education program may pursue a professional teaching career. With the required Professional Education units and completion of the program, students become qualified to take the LET and become licensed Physical Education teachers — opening opportunities in public and private schools, sports programs, and community education settings.

Lead with Excellence. Serve with Purpose. Teach with Passion.

If your passion is to promote health, inspire active lifestyles, and shape disciplined learners, the Bachelor of Physical Education at PIC is the perfect path to begin your career in teaching and sports leadership.
