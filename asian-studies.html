Bachelor of Arts in Asian Studies

Why choose Asian Studies

A One-of-a-Kind Program in the Philippines

Philippine International College (PIC) proudly offers the only Bachelor of Arts in Asian Studies major in Korea Studies program in the entire country. This unique degree is designed for students who want to specialize in Korean culture, language, society, business, and international relations.

Unlock High-Value Career Opportunities

Graduates of this program gain access to a wide range of local and international careers — including translation, tourism, business, diplomacy, cultural exchange, and employment in Korean companies. Because this field is highly in demand, students have strong earning potential and competitive career advantages.

International Partnerships With Korean Institutions

Our strong partnerships with universities and organizations in South Korea open doors to:

  • Exchange programs
  • Continuing education abroad
  • Internships
  • Industry Placements
  • Direct employment pathways

Study and Work in South Korea

PIC provides students with opportunities to continue their studies in South Korea while working, allowing them to gain real-world experience and earn income as they learn.

Your Global Career Starts Here

If you want to build a future connected to South Korea's dynamic culture and fast-growing industries, this program is your gateway.
