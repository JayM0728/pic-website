Welcome to God's College

Our Mission

Our mission is to train global Christian leaders to change the world. To prepare these spiritual leaders in Christian ministries and in their chosen profession, PIC seeks to offer theological and practical education in a holistic approach through:

  • Balance curriculum of biblical, practical and professional training.
  • Spiritual formation in all its activities through instruction, worship and service.
  • Active ministry involvement for an immediate practical application of their learning.

Our Vision

Philippine International College, an undergraduate and post graduate level institution exists to serve the Almighty God by producing highly qualified and competent ministers and leaders that would impact real living change to countless generations through the power of the Holy Spirit. This God-centered place of learning also generates godly spiritual leaders for the school's denomination, Philippine Evangelical Holiness Church and other mission oriented organizations.

Our Calling

PIC is God's college, a Christ-centered place of learning. God has given us vision and calling to educate global Christian leaders to change the world through the power of the Holy Spirit. For this calling, as a servant of this college, I will do my best today for God's glory.
