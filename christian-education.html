Bachelor of Arts in Christian Education

Prepare to Teach, Lead, and Serve with Excellence and Faith

Shaping Future Christian Educators and Leaders

The Bachelor of Arts in Christian Education at Philippine International College is designed for students who feel called to teach, guide, and influence the next generation. This program develops strong biblical foundations while equipping students with effective teaching skills for Christian schools and ministry settings.

A Strong Blend of Theology and Education

Students receive comprehensive training in both biblical studies and educational principles. Subjects include:

  • Foundations of Christian Education
  • Biblical Studies
  • Theology and Doctrine
  • Educational Psychology
  • Curriculum Development
  • Teaching Strategies
  • Classroom Management
  • Child and Adolescent Development
  • Values and Character Formation

With this combination, graduates become well-rounded educators rooted in faith and excellence.

Practical Training and Classroom Experience

Students grow through actual teaching exposure and ministry involvement such as:

  • Practice teaching in Christian schools
  • Sunday school teaching
  • Outreach programs
  • Curriculum workshops
  • Church and community educational ministries

These experiences build confidence and readiness for real-world teaching.

Pathways to Local and International Careers

Graduates can pursue roles such as:

  • Christian school teacher
  • Sunday school or children's ministry leader
  • Curriculum developer
  • Values education teacher
  • Missions educator
  • Church education director
  • Tutor or learning facilitator
  • Graduate studies in education or theology

Eligible to Take the LET (Licensure Examination for Teachers)

Graduates of BA in Christian Education may pursue a professional teaching career. With the required Professional Education units, students become qualified to take the LET and become licensed teachers — opening opportunities in private schools, Christian academies, and educational ministries.

Lead with Faith. Serve with Purpose. Teach with Passion.

If your passion is to educate, inspire, and shape lives, the Bachelor of Arts in Christian Education at PIC is the perfect path to begin your calling.
