About PIC

Training Global Christian Leaders Since 1995

Our Story

Philippine International College was founded in 1995 with a singular vision: to provide world-class education grounded in Christian values. What began as a small Bible school has grown into a fully accredited institution offering undergraduate and graduate programs across multiple disciplines.

Our commitment to "Training Global Christian Leaders" shapes everything we do - from our rigorous academic curriculum to our vibrant campus ministry and community outreach programs.

Mission

To provide quality Christian education that develops competent professionals and servant leaders who will impact the world for Christ.

Vision

To be a leading international Christian college recognized for academic excellence, spiritual formation, and global outreach.

Core Values

  • Faith - Christ-centered in all endeavors
  • Excellence - Pursuing the highest standards in education
  • Integrity - Living with honesty and moral courage
  • Service - Using knowledge and skills for the benefit of others
  • Global Perspective - Preparing leaders for an interconnected world
