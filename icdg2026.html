International Christian Development Gathering

PIC Hosts ICDG 2026

Philippine International College was honored to host the International Christian Development Gathering (ICDG) 2026 from March 15-18, 2026. This prestigious event brought together over 300 Christian educators, ministry leaders, and development professionals from more than 20 countries.

Conference Theme

"Training Global Christian Leaders for the Next Generation"

Key Highlights

  • Keynote addresses from renowned international speakers
  • Workshops on Christian education, missions, and community development
  • Networking sessions with global partner institutions
  • Cultural night showcasing Filipino Christian heritage
  • Signing of 5 new international partnership agreements

Participating Countries

Delegates attended from the Philippines, United States, South Korea, Japan, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, Australia, Kenya, Brazil, and many more.
